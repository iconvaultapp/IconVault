Our story

Built for the road.

From winding coastal highways and alpine passes to long-distance grand tours, the Axiom GT-X is made for the full spectrum of driving. Electric power gives you the freedom to drive the way you want, wherever you want.

Ready for the track

Thermal-managed battery packs and track-calibrated aero remove previous limits from any lap strategy. Push the pace without compromise. With carbon-ceramic brakes and a rigid monocoque, the GT-X inspires confidence to drive beyond your previous limits.

Built by engineers. Driven by enthusiasts.

This didn't start with a market opportunity or a category trend. It began with people who love to drive and who have spent their lives building things, breaking them, and improving them. Electric technology was never unfamiliar to us — it's always been part of how we think, design, and solve problems. The goal was never to translate a conventional car into electric form. The goal was to build a platform that fully embraces the possibilities of electric propulsion, without losing what drivers care about.
