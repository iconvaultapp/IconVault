Our Guides

Anchoring Wisdom & Somatic Awareness

Our teachers are long-term somatic practitioners dedicated to preserving traditional lineages while honoring functional contemporary biomechanics and neurological safety.

Lead Vinyasa & Prana Guide

Aria Vance

With over 12 years of formal training across Rishikesh and Mysore, Aria integrates traditional spiritual philosophy, fluid sequencing, and poetry in motion to awaken heart consciousness.

Hatha & Biomechanics Specialist

Kaelen Reed

Kaelen combines classical alignment-based yoga with modern clinical biomechanics. His classes empower students to master postural integrity and prevent repetitive strain.

Yin & Sound Therapist

A former classical modern dancer, Elena integrates neural-restorative Yin practices with quartz crystal and alchemy sound bath frequencies to facilitate profound cell-level somatic release.
