Frequently Asked

Got Questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know about setting up and scaling your SaaS template on Launch.

How does Launch achieve sub-10ms deployment latency?

We leverage an intelligent Global Edge Network utilizing over 48 point-of-presence (POP) data centers. When you deploy, your static files, HTML headers, and API databases are cached and served directly from the node closest to the requesting browser, reducing round-trip connection overhead significantly.

Can I migrate from Vercel, AWS, or Heroku automatically?

Absolutely. Our command-line tool (`launch`) scan and recognize active configuration profiles from alternative platforms automatically. In one click, we migrate database connection strings, parse environment variables, and build routing settings directly into your dashboard.

Is there a limit on bandwidth or database sizes?

Starter plans include soft-limit capacity warnings up to 50 GB. Pro Scale plans allow dynamic burstable bandwidth to prevent service disruptions. Enterprise tiers are totally uncapped with customized, isolated database architectures tailored exclusively for high throughput.

How secure is my data in the Multi-Cloud backup?

Your database backups are secure under AES-256 encryption both at rest and in transit. By distributing encrypted fragments dynamically across AWS S3, Google Cloud Storage, and Azure Blob storage, we ensure high durability and resistance to single-point-of-failure regional blackouts.

Do you offer custom SLAs for Enterprise tier setups?

Yes, our Enterprise packages feature contractual Service Level Agreements (SLAs) offering up to 99.99% uptime guarantees. You will be assigned a dedicated solutions engineer and gain 24/7 access to priority pager hotlines and shared Slack, Teams, or Discord coordination channels.
