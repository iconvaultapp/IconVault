Elena Vance

Editor & Essayist

"A design is not done when there is nothing left to add, but when there is nothing left to subtract."
Analog-first workflow
Absolute distraction-free interfaces
Longevity over short-term trends

I spend my days designing layout grids and structural systems for interfaces that respect human cognitive capacity. My essays explore the subtle art of subtracting physical and digital noise to rediscover our genuine voice.

Before launching this Minimal Journal, I worked as a typographical advisor for design firms across Scandinavia. Currently, I run a boutique publishing press and practice analog photography with medium-format cameras.
