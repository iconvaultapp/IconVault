Begin Your Journey

Veridian State University seeks inquisitive, dedicated minds from all backgrounds. We look beyond basic test metrics to assess your character, intellectual curiosity, and potential for leadership.

I

Choose Your Pathway

Select your entry type: Undergraduate first-year, transfer pathway, Graduate doctoral, or global international student status.

II

Gather Official Credentials

Secure three recommendation letters, academic transcripts, and prepare your personal philosophical statement of purpose.

Submit Before Dec 15th
